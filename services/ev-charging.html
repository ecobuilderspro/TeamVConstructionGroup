EV Charging Infrastructure

Team V has installed and commissioned thousands of EV charging ports across multifamily, workplace, fleet, and public sites. From Level 2 expansions to DC fast charging hubs, we handle service upgrades, conduit, equipment installation, networking, and full integration with solar, battery storage, and site logistics systems so that smart charging performs as a coordinated part of the larger energy infrastructure.

Selected Work

Kaiser Montebello – PowerFlex Level 2

Large workplace Level 2 installation · Medical campus

PowerFlex Level 2 charging installation supporting staff and visitor vehicles at the Kaiser Permanente Montebello medical offices. Scope included service coordination, equipment installation, and integration with site electrical infrastructure.

Getty Center – DC Fast Charging

150 kW Delta DCFC · Campus installation

Installation and commissioning support for a 150 kW Delta DC fast charger at the Getty Center campus, providing higher-power charging capability alongside existing Level 2 infrastructure.

UPS San Diego – Fleet Depot

Fleet charging · San Diego

Fleet depot charging infrastructure supporting electric delivery vehicles in San Diego. Focused on reliable, high-utilization charging for commercial fleet operations.
