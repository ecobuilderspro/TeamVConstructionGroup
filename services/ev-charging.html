EV Charging Infrastructure

Level 1, Level 2 and DC fast-charging construction for multifamily, commercial, fleet and workplace applications. We handle everything from service upgrades and conduit to installation, networking and commissioning.

Selected Work

Multifamily Level 2 Expansion – Orange County

32 dual-port Level 2 stations · Existing garage retrofit

Upgraded electrical service and installed 32 dual-port Level 2 chargers across two parking structures for a 280-unit apartment community. Included load management, networking, and close coordination with the utility.

Workplace DC Fast Charging Hub

4 × 150 kW DCFC · Corporate campus

Designed and constructed a four-stall DC fast-charging hub for employee and visitor use. Scope covered trenching, concrete pads, switchgear, and full startup testing.

Fleet Depot Level 2 Build-Out

48 ports · Municipal fleet yard

Installed a 48-port Level 2 system for a municipal light-duty fleet. Work included new transformers, underground feeders, and integration with the owner’s existing charge-management platform.
